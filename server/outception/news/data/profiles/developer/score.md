---
levels:
  "0": not for developers at all
  "1": a passing mention of technology
  "2": industry colour with no consequence for how software gets built
  "3": minor product news or a small release
  "4": a notable release or an incident affecting few developers
  "5": a release or change many developers will notice
  "6": a change most developers on that platform must know about
  "7": a security issue or platform change that demands action this week
  "8": a major release, breach or policy shift across the industry
  "9": a change that alters how a large share of software is built
  "10": a once-a-year event for the whole profession
---
Score by consequence for a working developer, not by hype. A critical vulnerability in a widely deployed library outranks any funding round. A new model, framework or language version scores by how many developers must change something because of it. Marketing announcements without a shipped artifact score low. Do not equate popularity with importance.
