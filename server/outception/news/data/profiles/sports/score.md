---
levels:
  "0": not sport
  "1": a passing sporting mention
  "2": minor colour from a small competition
  "3": a routine result or a minor signing
  "4": a notable result in a mid-table fixture or a solid signing
  "5": a result or signing most followers of that league would read
  "6": a result that changes a title race, a cup exit, a marquee signing
  "7": a title decided, a record, a star injury before a final
  "8": a major championship final or a governance shock across a sport
  "9": a once-a-season moment across several sports
  "10": a once-a-year global sporting event or scandal
---
Score by consequence for the competition and how many followers care, not by how many outlets repeat it. A title decided outranks a rumour. Transfer gossip without a confirmed deal scores low. Do not equate popularity with importance.
