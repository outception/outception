---
levels:
  "0": nothing an investor would act on
  "1": a passing mention of money or business
  "2": colour with no price consequence
  "3": a small company update or a routine data point
  "4": a notable update for one company or a small sector
  "5": news most holders of one large company or sector would read
  "6": a result, deal or data release that moves a major stock or sector
  "7": a central bank, regulatory or earnings event that moves a market
  "8": a shock to a whole asset class or economy
  "9": a change that reprices most portfolios
  "10": a once-a-year event for global markets
---
Score by how much and how broadly prices or outlooks move, not by how loud the coverage is. A guidance cut from a bellwether outranks a celebrity listing. Routine daily moves without a cause score low. Do not equate popularity with importance.
