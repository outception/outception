---
levels:
  "0": not news
  "1": trivia
  "2": local colour with no wider consequence
  "3": a routine development in an ongoing story
  "4": a notable development for one country or sector
  "5": a story most readers in one country would read
  "6": a story most readers in several countries would read
  "7": a major event: an election result, a verdict, a disaster, a treaty
  "8": a shock with global consequences
  "9": a once-a-year event
  "10": a once-a-decade event
---
Score by consequence and reach, not by how many outlets repeat it. A verdict that changes a law outranks a viral moment. Do not equate popularity with importance.
