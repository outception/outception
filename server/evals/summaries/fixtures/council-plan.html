Council backs 12 million riverside plan after week of talks

The city council voted on Thursday to back a 12 million plan to rebuild the riverside walk, ending a week of talks between the two largest parties over the cost of the scheme and the loss of forty parking spaces.

The vote passed by nineteen to twelve with three abstentions. Opponents said the plan did too little for the estates on the far bank and promised to raise the question of parking again when the detailed design is published next spring.

The council leader said work would start in the autumn and finish within two years, with the first stretch of the walk open before the summer festival.
