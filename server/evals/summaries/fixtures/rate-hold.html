Central bank holds rates at 4.25 percent

The central bank held its main rate at 4.25 percent on Wednesday, as most economists had expected, and said it would watch the labour market closely before deciding on any change later in the year.

Two of the nine members voted for a cut. The bank's statement said inflation had fallen faster than forecast in the spring but that wage growth remained above the level consistent with its target.

Markets had priced in a hold. The currency was little changed after the decision and bond yields fell slightly.
