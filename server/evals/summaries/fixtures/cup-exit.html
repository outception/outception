Holders out of the cup on penalties in quarter-final

The holders are out of the cup after losing a quarter-final shoot-out on penalties, having drawn 2-2 after extra time against a side two divisions below them.

The visitors scored twice in the last ten minutes of normal time to force the extra period. Their keeper saved the fourth penalty, and the fifth was struck over the bar.

The manager said his side had been the better team for an hour and had paid for two lapses.
