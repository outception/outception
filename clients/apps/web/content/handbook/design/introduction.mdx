Outception is a live wall of headlines. The design job is to get a reader from
opening the app to the story they want with nothing in the way: no account, no
onboarding, no chrome competing with the headlines.

## Governing principle

Design by subtraction. Start from the headline and the source, add only what a
reader needs to scan, filter and move on. Nothing decorative.

## What the product should feel like

- On first open: a full wall of current headlines, readable immediately.
- After a few minutes: fast. Swiping the deck, switching topics and following
  sources never waits on the network.
- Over time: dependable. The wall is always current and never surprises the
  reader with a layout change.
