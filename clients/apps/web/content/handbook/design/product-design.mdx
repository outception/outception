title: 'Product Design'
description: 'How the design principles apply to the deck and the wall'
---

## The headline is the content

Everything else on screen supports it: source name, time, topic. Typography and
spacing exist to make a column of headlines scannable. Images are optional and
never displace text.

## Density

Readers come to see a lot at once. The wall shows many headlines per screen and
trusts the reader to scan. Hierarchy comes from type size and grouping by
topic, not from hiding things behind extra taps.

## Every state is a real state

Empty deck, no results, offline, source unavailable: each one is designed with
the same care as the wall. An empty deck tells the reader how to add sources.
An error says what happened and what to do next.

## Speed

Headlines render before anything else. Filtering and swiping are local. A tap
on a headline opens the source directly. Any added latency is a design
regression, not a technical detail.

## No account required

Reading never asks for sign-in. An account only appears when it adds something:
syncing a deck across devices, or managing an organization.
